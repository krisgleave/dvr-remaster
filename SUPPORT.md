## Reporting a problem

When reporting a problem, please include:

- DVR Remaster version
- Operating system
- Computer and processor
- FPV video system
- Original recording format and resolution
- Description of the issue
- Steps to reproduce the issue

Where possible, provide a short sample recording that demonstrates the problem.

Please avoid sharing footage containing private or sensitive information.
